Overlook Looks

Palettes and layouts rendered on scrubbed copies of your Outlook, captured with the DevTools snippet so Outlook's own styles, fonts and icons are intact. Names and text are placeholder words. The Calm shots have Outlook's "Move to" menu open, which shows that menus and popups pick up the palette too.

About the layouts

  • Calm keeps Outlook's three panes and quiets the chrome: no ribbon tabs, softer app rail, flatter rows.
  • Fastmail is list first: full-width rows, a sidebar of folders, a flat toolbar with an accent compose button. It assumes Outlook's Reading pane: Hide setting so a message opens full width.
  • Focus drops the app rail, folder pane and ribbon, and centres the list. Switch folders with g. Same reading-pane setting.
  • Dark palettes show email bodies as a light card, since most emails hard-code a white background.
